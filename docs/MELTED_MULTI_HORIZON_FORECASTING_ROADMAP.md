# Melted Gold Multi-Horizon Forecasting Roadmap

## Status and decision

**Status: `DEFERRED — RESEARCH DESIGN ONLY`.**

This document records the intended design for a future multi-horizon forecast
system centred on Iranian melted gold, especially the paper market.  It does
not enable a collector, scheduler, model, remote AI provider, database write,
runtime inference path, or user-facing output.

It is deliberately **not the current priority**.  The active product priority
remains the coin-price-estimation feature and its existing Shadow contracts.
The forecast programme must wait for a materially longer, quality-controlled,
chronological market history.  It must not consume effort or production
authority from the coin-estimation work until its stage gates are met and a
separate owner decision re-opens it.

This roadmap extends, but does not alter, these existing Shadow-only contracts:

- `MELTED_MARKET_RELATIONSHIP_DISCOVERY_SHADOW.md`;
- `COIN_PRICE_INTELLIGENCE_SHADOW_ROADMAP.md`; and
- `COIN_PRICE_INTELLIGENCE_SHADOW_V2.md`.

Those contracts remain authoritative for their current boundaries.  In
particular, a high-volume paper-melted signal must never dilute the direct
evidential weight of a fresh, confirmed coin trade.

## Product objective

The future system forecasts the *conditional distribution* of a market, not a
single guaranteed price and not a buy/sell instruction.  At a strict,
point-in-time cutoff it must answer, for one target segment and one future
horizon:

- the current valid anchor and its age;
- expected return and expected price centre;
- a lower/central/upper range (for example P10/P50/P90);
- probabilities of up, down and range outcomes;
- current market regime and confidence;
- important evidence groups and explicit data-quality warnings; and
- whether the model abstained and which safe baseline was used instead.

The first target is **unconditional normal paper melted gold**, with today and
tomorrow settlement treated as separate target cells.  Reverse and swim paper,
physical melted gold, conditional observations, dollar, USDT, ounce, exchange
gold/coin and coin observations are independent explanatory series unless a
future stage explicitly validates a transfer relation.

The programme later transfers verified insight from paper melted gold to
physical melted gold and then to coin estimation.  It does not skip directly
from generic market data to a product-specific coin prediction.

## Non-goals and invariants

- No model may fabricate a price for a missing source or relabel USDT as
  Herat.  Any bridge is explicit, labelled estimated, and carries its source
  age and uncertainty.
- No LLM is the numeric forecaster or the automatic weight controller.  LLMs
  may later turn public news into structured, auditable exogenous features.
- Raw Telegram text, source links, message identifiers, participant names,
  phone numbers and account identifiers are excluded from research features,
  model bundles and forecast ledgers.
- A forecast never trains on its own prediction or on an outcome unavailable at
  its cutoff.
- An offer or trade that fails the existing market-quality gate stays excluded
  from both live evidence and training evidence as specified by the current
  contract.
- Conditional physical offers remain separate evidence.  They cannot silently
  teach the ordinary, unconditional physical-price relationship.
- Every prospective runtime effect is Shadow-only until a later, versioned
  human approval.  There is no automatic promotion or coefficient write-back.

## Forecast horizons

Each horizon has a separate target and may have a separate model.  A longer
forecast must not be generated by repeatedly applying a minute model, because
that compounds error and loses the calendar state at the destination.

| Tier | Forecast horizons | Principal use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| micro | 1, 5, 15, 30, 60 minutes | order flow and near-term movement |
| intraday | 2, 4, 6 hours; market-session close | session, basis and cross-market movement |
| next-day | next opening; next session close | overnight, calendar and reopening risk |
| multi-day | 2, 3, 5, 7 trading days | calendar, holiday, trend and accumulated regime |

For every horizon, the dataset contains three independently scoreable labels:

1. realised return relative to the last valid same-segment anchor;
2. direction: `UP`, `DOWN`, or `RANGE`, with the deadband versioned per
   horizon; and
3. realised conditional quantiles/range for uncertainty calibration.

The label's realised observation must be strictly later than the cutoff and
must pass a horizon-specific maximum-staleness rule.  The system records the
actual elapsed duration as well as the nominal horizon, so market closures and
missing observations cannot masquerade as a normal five-minute outcome.

## Stage 1 — point-in-time market data contract

### Purpose

Make the data suitable for future forecasting without yet fitting a forecast
model.

### Inputs retained as independent axes

| Input family | Required distinctions |
| --- | --- |
| paper melted gold | normal/reverse/swim/generic; today/tomorrow; offer/trade; buy/sell; quantity |
| physical melted gold | today/tomorrow; non-conditional/conditional; offer/trade; buy/sell; quantity |
| dollar | Herat versus other source; physical/cash versus paper; settlement; direct versus explicitly bridged |
| external references | USDT, XAUUSD, standardized exchange gold and coin, each with own unit, time and source age |
| coin evidence | canonical commodity, cash/tomorrow, offer/trade, quality status; used as a later transfer target, not a paper-melted substitute |

### Required row metadata

Every normalized observation and aggregate must retain:

- event time, availability time and Tehran time;
- Gregorian and Jalali date keys;
- feed/source quality and staleness state: `FRESH`, `STALE`, `MISSING`,
  `ESTIMATED`, or `REFERENCE_ONLY`;
- parser/quality decision version;
- revision and deduplication key; and
- explicit data lineage without raw-message identifiers in model tables.

### Stage gate

Before progressing, the data audit must demonstrate:

- no same-timestamp or future observation is available to a feature row;
- no source substitution under another source's name;
- deduplication and edit handling are idempotent;
- the target paper cells have enough valid observations across distinct Tehran
  days, openings and closures; and
- data-quality and missingness rates are measured rather than assumed.

## Stage 2 — Iranian market calendar and session contract

### Purpose

Represent information that is known before a forecast horizon begins.

### Calendar features

- Tehran minute-of-day and market session;
- weekday and weekend state;
- Jalali day, month and year;
- beginning, middle and end-of-month indicators;
- official Iranian holidays, Thursday banking/market conditions, Friday
  closure, pre-holiday and post-holiday state;
- distance to each market opening and closing time;
- domestic banking availability;
- global-gold market open/closed state and time since its last valid quote;
- recurring seasonal/calendar cohorts, including Nowruz where enough history
  exists.

The common market observation that early-month and late-month behaviour can
differ is a testable calendar feature, not a hard-coded direction rule.  It is
allowed to influence a forecast only when later chronological evidence shows a
stable incremental benefit beyond the market baseline.

### Stage gate

The calendar adapter must be versioned, reproducible from an offline official
holiday source, testable around year boundaries, and explicitly able to report
unknown/ad-hoc closure state.  It must never silently mark an unknown day as a
normal session.

## Stage 3 — durable multi-resolution feature store

### Purpose

Generate only evidence that was observable at each cutoff.

### Feature families

For each target segment and 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, 30, 60 and 180-minute lookback,
build the following source-separated aggregate features:

- latest valid price, weighted price and age;
- returns, acceleration, volatility and range;
- offer and confirmed-trade count, quantity and trade share;
- buy/sell imbalance for offers and trades separately;
- directional sequence length and order-flow/trade-flow persistence;
- same-kind today/tomorrow basis;
- paper/physical basis, with conditional physical excluded by default;
- cross-market returns, lead/lag candidates and disagreement;
- source freshness/missingness and input-coverage signature; and
- Stage-2 calendar/session features.

Incompatible-unit price levels are not combined into artificial spreads.
Normalized, dimensionally valid ratios and returns are allowed only when their
source and timestamp contract is explicit.

### Retention

Raw ingestion data may follow the existing limited-retention policy.  The
durable research ledger retains privacy-minimized, point-in-time numeric
feature/target rows and quality metadata.  It must preserve events and counts
needed to learn order-flow behaviour; it must not reduce all activity to a
single daily mean.

### Stage gate

The feature builder must pass point-in-time replay tests, timestamp-boundary
tests, missing-source tests and unit-consistency tests.  A sampled historic
cutoff must be reproducible byte-for-byte from the durable ledger.

## Stage 4 — safe baselines and regime model

### Baselines

Each target/horizon is always evaluated against transparent baselines:

1. last valid same-segment price;
2. same-segment short-window weighted price/return;
3. last anchor adjusted only by independently observed compatible melted flow;
4. calendar-only baseline; and
5. a deliberately conservative abstention/range baseline when input quality is
   insufficient.

### Regime model

Train and validate an independent regime classifier/regressor that reports at
least:

- `RANGE` / ordinary;
- `UP`;
- `DOWN`;
- `VOLATILE`;
- `REOPENING`;
- `PRE_OR_POST_HOLIDAY`; and
- `SHOCK_OR_DISAGREEMENT`.

It is led by paper-melted flow and price behaviour, with dollar, USDT,
physical melted, global gold and standardized exchange references as explicit
support.  Coin order flow can validate liquidity and tolerance, but cannot
lead the underlying melted-market direction.

### Stage gate

The regime candidate must improve either calibrated forecast error or interval
coverage against the no-regime counterpart in multiple untouched chronological
folds.  A regime label that merely describes the same future move is rejected
as leakage.

## Stage 5 — relationship discovery laboratory

### Methods

The following research models remain CPU-feasible and Shadow-only:

- CatBoost/LightGBM for non-linear tabular interactions and feature importance;
- symbolic regression for interpretable, dimensionally reviewed candidate
  equations;
- residual models for the error left by structural/baseline forecasts; and
- lightweight online Bayesian/Kalman-style residual calibration after enough
  realised, reviewed evidence exists.

Research questions include:

- Which paper variant and settlement leads a move in the target cell?
- When does today/tomorrow basis predict continuation, reversal or merely
  liquidity stress?
- How do conditional/non-conditional physical-paper gaps behave by regime?
- How do buy/sell run length, trade count and trade share alter short-horizon
  continuation probability?
- Under what calendar/session conditions do USDT and Herat movements transfer
  differently into paper melted gold?

### Acceptance rule

No discovered relationship is promoted solely on correlation, a single week,
or one regime.  It must have stable sign/effect across chronological
fit/validation/test partitions, adequate distinct Tehran-day support, a
material improvement over the relevant baseline, and a human-readable report
of its failure modes.

## Stage 6 — direct multi-horizon forecast challengers

### Model structure

Train one direct challenger per target cell/horizon, or a shared model with an
explicit horizon target and strict horizon-specific evaluation.  Do not use
recursive minute forecasts to create day forecasts.

The first candidates predict:

- expected return;
- P10/P50/P90 return or price quantiles; and
- probability of direction classes.

The prediction bundle must retain its dataset cutoff, feature schema version,
calendar version, model version, target segment, horizon and training-window
provenance.

### Uncertainty

Uncertainty must increase, never decrease by assumption, when:

- the anchor is older;
- source coverage is poorer;
- the market is closed or reopening after a gap;
- the observed regime is volatile or conflicting; or
- the prediction horizon is longer.

Conformal or equivalent held-out residual calibration must be fitted only on a
chronologically prior calibration partition.  An untouched later test measures
coverage.  A narrow but under-covering interval fails.

### Stage gate

For each proposed target/horizon, the challenger must beat the best safe
baseline in the later untouched test cohort on pre-declared metrics, while
meeting the locked interval-coverage target.  Results must be sliced by
regime, session, holiday state, input coverage and anchor age.  Sparse cells
remain `GATED_OFF`.

## Stage 7 — news and shock feature layer

### Boundary

This is optional and later than the numeric core.  A language model may
process only public, approved news content into a compact structured event:

- category (political, economic, currency, global gold, banking, exchange,
  sanctions, etc.);
- expected direction and intensity;
- expected duration;
- source credibility and event/publication time; and
- duplicate/contradictory-news state.

Raw news text does not enter the numerical bundle.  The forecast model treats
the structured event as an exogenous feature and must still require observed
market confirmation.  News alone cannot force a price jump or override a
strong contradictory market signal.

### Stage gate

The news layer must demonstrate incremental, out-of-sample benefit during
documented event windows without degrading ordinary-day performance.  If it
does not, it remains an explanatory annotation only.

## Stage 8 — shadow operations, monitoring and human promotion

### Shadow operation

Forecasts are written to a separate privacy-minimized forecast/outcome ledger
with `is_authoritative=false`.  They do not change offers, trades, price
ranges, parser outputs, customer-facing prices, weights or cross-server
business events.

Every prediction later links to a realised outcome only if the outcome was
strictly after its cutoff.  It records error, direction accuracy, interval
coverage, input coverage, regime, anchor age and abstention reason.

### Monitoring

Monitor at least:

- data freshness and missingness per source;
- ingestion/parser/quality rejection rates;
- forecast volume by target/horizon;
- MAE/RMSE and direction accuracy;
- interval coverage and interval width;
- drift in returns, bases, calendar cohorts and feature distributions;
- performance by normal, up, down, volatile, reopening and holiday cohorts;
- baseline-versus-challenger gap; and
- error after news/shock labels.

### Promotion rule

There is no auto-promotion.  A candidate requires a versioned report,
independent later test evidence, an explicit risk review, a rollback-ready
immutable bundle and named human approval.  Initial activation, if ever
approved, is observational or advisory; it cannot control trading behaviour.

## Stage 9 — controlled transfer to physical melted and coin models

Only after paper-melted models clear the relevant Stage-8 gates:

1. validate paper-to-physical transfer separately for unconditional physical
   today and tomorrow;
2. model cash/tomorrow basis by banking/session state without relabelling one
   as the other;
3. use physical 750-fineness melted anchors as the principal intrinsic input
   for low-date coins, with explicit paper fallback and uncertainty when the
   physical anchor is stale;
4. retain separate bubble/residual models per premium coin, settlement and
   evidence cell; and
5. prove improvement against the existing coin estimator under frozen-anchor,
   reopening and holiday scenarios before any future promotion discussion.

Fresh, confirmed same-commodity coin trades remain the strongest direct local
evidence.  Underlying-market forecasts help most when coin evidence is sparse,
closed or stale; they do not replace valid fresh coin evidence.

## Data-readiness thresholds (initial, reviewable)

These are entry gates for experiments, not promises that a model is ready for
production:

| Research layer | Minimum evidence before running |
| --- | --- |
| micro-horizon challenger | sufficient point-in-time rows across at least 30 distinct Tehran market days, including multiple openings and closures |
| daily forecast challenger | at least 6 months with multiple Jalali month boundaries, holidays and reopening cohorts |
| multi-day / seasonal candidate | at least 12 months, including one complete Iranian calendar cycle and recurring holiday periods |
| online residual calibration | enough strictly prior, reviewed outcomes per target/horizon to demonstrate stability, not merely volume |
| news layer | timestamped public news plus enough labelled event/non-event windows to establish incremental value |

The exact numeric row thresholds, coverage targets and maximum staleness must
be versioned in the experiment configuration before an experiment runs.  They
must not be lowered after seeing an unfavourable test result.

## Prioritisation and re-entry criteria

The work is intentionally paused after documentation.  It may be re-opened
only when all of the following are true:

1. coin-estimation feature work no longer needs the same engineering capacity;
2. the input pipeline has accumulated the necessary data-quality history;
3. feature-store retention and privacy boundaries are verified;
4. a product owner explicitly authorizes a new Shadow-only stage; and
5. an experiment specification states target cell, horizon, baseline, training
   cutoff, evaluation folds, success criteria and rollback/no-promotion rule.

Until then, collection should preserve the source distinctions and
point-in-time numeric aggregates described here.  That preserves future
optionality without prematurely turning a small, short history into a
misleading forecasting system.
