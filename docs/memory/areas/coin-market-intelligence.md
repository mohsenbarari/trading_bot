# Coin Market Intelligence

- USD/Herat repair infers omitted leading digits from three prior same-source/book
  facts within 15 minutes; never add a constant. Replay chronologically.
- In the private group feed, `خ ن ف`/`ف ن ف`, `خ ف`/`ف ف`, and marker-less
  side posts mean tomorrow; `خ ن`/`ف ن` and `نق`/cash words mean cash. Future
  wins. Do not apply this feed default to user registration syntax.
- Coin-group prices may be project-thousand, full-Toman divided once by 1,000,
  or a bounded redundant-zero candidate; never add a constant. Quantities,
  scripts and mint years must not corrupt fields. `رب` means quarter;
  `پ`/`ت پ` and `پایین` variants mark low-date.
- Commodity resolution never defaults to Imam or restores
  `group_commodity_context`. Use prior same-book anchors no older than two hours;
  overlapping full-coin bands require coverage for every plausible book. Regime
  bootstrap needs a 30-minute explicit cluster: three messages, two
  senders, one nonconditional claim, 1.5% spread. Conditional claims only support;
  eligible facts propagate, and only canonical evidence rejects an explicit name.
- A trade requires one complete, structurally linked reply branch. Isolate
  users/siblings, choose the oldest root offer, and require reciprocal offerer
  evidence. Cancellation breaks inherited terms; later rejection gates the fill.
  Use final negotiated quantity/exact price, deduplicate, and link to the opaque
  root. Ambiguous, overfilled, or unresolved-root trades stay out of the model.
- Reconciliation rejects facts invalidated by edits/current reply graph;
  unchanged decisions retain first availability. Projection removes absent or
  rejected facts, but retains pending/conditional/over-five-minute-late facts for
  audit with zero eligibility/weight. Models use `available_at_utc`; reports also
  retain source event time.
- Reject malformed envelopes individually; inverted timestamps cannot poison
  checkpoints. Health separates heartbeat, latest canonical event,
  and model-eligible input; historical rows are not live.
- Private group text stays only in bounded external staging. Market Store and
  projection keep opaque normalized facts; never revive the legacy data plane.
  Live jobs use canonical `main`; retarget systemd before worktree removal.
- Estimator runtime state lives under
  `/srv/trading-bot/production-data/coin-intelligence/estimator-live`. The home
  page shows primary output and CASH/TOMORROW inputs; shadow/realised detail
  stays on `/shadow`.
- The web UI is the operator's observable contract for parser and estimator
  behavior. Its ledger shows every normalized offer/trade, separates
  model inputs from audit-only facts, and joins eligible facts to the prediction
  ledger for main/shadow prices and cycle times—never recomputation.
- Input health separates heartbeat from market-hours freshness, excludes stale
  inputs, prefers normalized sources, and keeps live-only proxies out of training.
